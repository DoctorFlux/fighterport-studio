# Material pipeline

Current preview resolves native MDL0 sampler references through owning BRRES and eligible ARC texture banks. WPF displays decoded TEX0 color/alpha on sampler 0 / UV0, with a neutral checker fallback. Explicit texture/palette overrides persist in FPWORK. Texture viewer exposes native format, dimensions, mips and material usages. Indexed CI8/RGB5A3 alpha and two mips are tested with original native fixtures; additional formats and mixed-axis wrap remain incomplete. Native TEV/alpha-test/multisampler composition is not reproduced. No destination style fidelity claim.

UV evaluation supports direct Maya scale/translation and XSI/3dsMax scale/rotation/translation. Native fractional SRT0 matrices and independent quarter-turn expectations are checked. Pinned BrawlLib Matrix34.MayaMtxR writes its vertical offset to d[6] rather than d[7]; rotation-only results differ from the general centered formula. Maya rotation remains explicitly unsupported until independently resolved, rather than propagating an unverified correction. Additional UV sets and indirect samplers remain retained in source, not rendered.

REQ-08 includes four non-destructive style preset families, regions, custom normals/textures/palettes/ramps/masks/vertex colors/transparency, packing/color spaces and supported bake routes. Rivals ORMS R-outline/G-roughness/B-metallic/A-shading and Mask compression were verified against the official current guide; see REFERENCE_INSPECTION.md. Implementation, base-color sRGB and installed Modkit behavior remain unverified.

Latest: canonical-to-bitmap conversion and independent U/V addressing are implemented with bounded affine image domains; native asymmetric color/alpha rendered regressions pass. WPF nearest requests measure as bilinear, and native LOD/TEV remain unsupported. Additional native UV arrays retained; direct sampler-0 UV0 only in preview. See TEXTURE_DIAGNOSIS.md for confirmed defects versus hypotheses.
