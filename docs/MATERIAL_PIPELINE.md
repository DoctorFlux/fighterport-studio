# Material pipeline

Current preview resolves native MDL0 sampler references through owning BRRES and eligible ARC texture banks. WPF displays decoded TEX0 color/alpha on one selected direct sampler/native UV (default sampler 0), with a neutral checker fallback. Explicit texture/palette overrides persist in FPWORK. Texture viewer exposes native format, dimensions, mips and material usages. Indexed CI8/RGB5A3 alpha and two mips are tested with original native fixtures; additional formats remain incomplete; independent U/V wrapping has rendered regressions. Native TEV/alpha-test/multisampler composition is not reproduced. No destination style fidelity claim.

UV evaluation supports direct Maya scale/translation and XSI/3dsMax scale/rotation/translation. Native fractional SRT0 matrices and independent quarter-turn expectations are checked. Pinned BrawlLib Matrix34.MayaMtxR writes its vertical offset to d[6] rather than d[7]; rotation-only results differ from the general centered formula. Maya rotation remains explicitly unsupported until independently resolved, rather than propagating an unverified correction. Available additional UV arrays are retained canonically and can be inspected individually. Indirect samplers remain preserved and unevaluated.

REQ-08 includes four non-destructive style preset families, regions, custom normals/textures/palettes/ramps/masks/vertex colors/transparency, packing/color spaces and supported bake routes. Rivals ORMS R-outline/G-roughness/B-metallic/A-shading and Mask compression were verified against the official current guide; see REFERENCE_INSPECTION.md. Implementation, base-color sRGB and installed Modkit behavior remain unverified.
